About Kanchwale

All glass needs in one place · Made in India, for India, by Indians

Our Story

Kanchwale is India's premier glass and mirror manufacturer with facilities in Mumbai, Indore, Visakhapatnam, Chennai and Bangalore. Founded with a passion for precision and design, we craft every piece in our own facilities — no middlemen, no compromises.

Our Promise

When you order from Kanchwale, you're ordering directly from the manufacturer. Better quality, faster customisation, and prices that reflect real value.

What We Offer

  • Decorative & designer mirrors
  • LED smart mirrors
  • Glass partitions & shower enclosures
  • Glass railings & balustrades
  • Custom glass works of all types
  • Corporate gifting solutions
